About CE2CT

The inaugural edition of the "CE2CT" conference series is set to take place at the Graphic Era Hill University (GEHU) Bhimtal Campus in Bhimtal, Nainital, Uttarakhand, India, on February 21-22, 2025. The conference "CE2CT" is being organized under the auspices of the IEEE Uttar Pradesh Section. This conference aims to provide a platform for researchers, academics, industry professionals, and students to share their latest research findings and innovations in the fields of Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Electronics, and Communication Technologies. Key highlights of the conference may include:

Venue: Graphic Era Hill University (GEHU) Bhimtal Campus, located in the scenic town of Bhimtal, Nainital, Uttarakhand, providing an inspiring backdrop for the exchange of ideas and knowledge.

Dates: The conference is scheduled for February 21-22, 2025, offering participants an intensive two-day program of presentations, discussions, and networking opportunities.

Scope: The conference will cover a wide range of topics within the domains of Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Electronics, and Communication Technologies, fostering interdisciplinary collaboration and exploration of emerging trends and challenges.

IEEE Affiliation: The conference is approved by the IEEE Uttar Pradesh Section, indicating its adherence to IEEE standards and guidelines, and ensuring high-quality content and professional organization.

Participants: Researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students from around the world are invited to participate, presenting their research findings, sharing insights, and engaging in discussions aimed at advancing knowledge and innovation in the field.

CE2CT, 2025

The vision behind organising this conference is to provide an excellent forum for researchers, scientist and industrialist from interdisciplinary areas to showcase their current contribution in the recent area of Physical Sciences.
