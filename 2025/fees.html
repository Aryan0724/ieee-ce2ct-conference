• The conference organizer motivates the participants to take part the conference in PHYSICAL MODE in order to utilise the fullest benefits of conference participation. The registration fee excludes any applicable bank charges.
• Please note that the registration fee is non-refundable. Once paid, it will not be refunded under any circumstances and no requests for refunds will be entertained, as the registration secures resources and commitments made by the organizing team.
• To avail of the registration benefits under the IEEE member category, a valid IEEE membership card for the year 2025 is required.

• The conference registration fee provides comprehensive access to the event's offerings. It includes full participation across all conference days, certificates for paper presentations or general attendance, and access to both technical and social sessions. For in-person attendees, the fee also covers daily conference lunches, exclusive entry to cultural events, and a local excursion (if any) organized by the conference committee.
