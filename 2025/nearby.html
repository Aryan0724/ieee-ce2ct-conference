Nearby Places

Bhimtal, Nainital, and the surrounding areas offer a plethora of attractions for visitors to explore. Here are some nearby places you might consider visiting while attending the event at the Graphic Era Hill University (GEHU) Bhimtal Campus:





Bhimtal Lake

Located in the heart of Bhimtal, this lake is famous for its island with an aquarium. You can enjoy boat rides and appreciate the serene beauty of the area.



Nainital Lake

Just an hour away, Nainital is known for its stunning Naini Lake, bustling Mall Road, and the panoramic views from Snow View Point. The Nainital Zoo and Naina Devi Temple are other attractions here.



Naukuchital

This lake with nine corners is perfect for boating, paragliding, and fishing. It’s also a great spot for nature walks and photography.



Sattal

A group of seven freshwater lakes amidst dense forests, Sattal is ideal for bird watching, boating, and hiking. It's a peaceful spot away from the crowds.



Nantin Baba Ashram

The Nantin Baba Ashram is a lesser-known spiritual retreat located in the serene Kumaon region, which adds a tranquil experience for visitors seeking peace and meditation.



Hidimba Parvat

This hill is named after Hidimba, the wife of Bhima from the Mahabharata. It offers panoramic views of the surrounding hills and is a popular spot for trekking and picnics.



Neem Karoli Baba Ashram Kainchi Dham

A popular pilgrimage site, Kainchi Dham ashram is known for its serene atmosphere and is associated with Neem Karoli Baba, making it a spiritual stop for many.



Ghorakhal Temple

Located near Bhimtal, this temple is dedicated to Lord Golu, an incarnation of Lord Ganesha. The temple complex also houses a beautiful garden and offers panoramic views of the surrounding valleys.



Ranikhet

This hill station is known for its lush green meadows, colonial architecture, and the Jhula Devi Temple. Ranikhet also offers great views of the Himalayas.



Almora

About a couple of hours away, Almora is rich in cultural heritage with several temples, scenic views, and a vibrant local market.

These sites offer a blend of nature, adventure, and spirituality, making your trip to the Bhimtal campus of Graphic Era Hill University a perfect opportunity for exploration.
