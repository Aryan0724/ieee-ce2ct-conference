Conference Scope

The scope of the "1st International Conference on Advances in Computer Science, Electrical, Electronics and Communication Technologies" (CE2CT) is likely to encompass a wide range of topics related to advancements and innovations in these fields.

We invite original research contributions related to the following pivotal fields:

Track 1. Computer Science and Engineering

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
Data Science and Big Data Analytics
Cybersecurity and Cryptography
Software Engineering and Development
Cloud Computing and Distributed Systems
Quantum Computing
Human-Computer Interaction

Track 2. Electrical Engineering

Power Systems and Smart Grids
Renewable Energy Technologies
Electrical Machines and Drives
Power Electronics and Applications
Electromagnetic Compatibility
Electrical Safety and Standards

Track 3. Electronics Engineering

VLSI Design and Nanoelectronics
Embedded Systems and IoT
Analog and Digital Circuits
Microelectronics and MEMS
Photonics and Optoelectronics
Sensor Networks and Applications

Track 4. Communication Technologies

5G/6G Wireless Communications
Optical and Satellite Communications
Signal Processing and Applications
Network Security and Management
IoT and M2M Communication
Cognitive Radio and Spectrum Management

Track 5. Control, Computing and Automation

Robotics and Autonomous Systems
Control System Design and Analysis
Process Control and Automation
Industrial Automation and Mechatronics
Cyber-Physical Systems
AI in Control and Automation
Computing technologies with engineering applications

Track 6. Interdisciplinary and Emerging Technologies

Bioinformatics and Biomedical Engineering
Smart Cities and Urban Computing
Green Technologies and Sustainable Development
Intelligent Transportation Systems
Nanotechnology in Electronics and Computing
Augmented and Virtual Reality
Blockchain and Distributed Ledger Technology
Edge Computing and IoT Integration
Wearable Technology and Smart Devices
Digital Twins and Simulation
AI Ethics and Societal Impacts
Space Technology and Satellite Systems
Decision making analysis

The conference might serve as a platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students to present their latest research findings, exchange ideas, and discuss emerging trends and challenges in these interdisciplinary fields.

CE2CT, 2025

The vision behind organising this conference is to provide an excellent forum for researchers, scientist and industrialist from interdisciplinary areas to showcase their current contribution in the recent area of Physical Sciences.
