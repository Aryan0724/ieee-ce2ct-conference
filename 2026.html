About the Second Edition

The Second International Conference on Advances in Computer Science, Electrical, Electronics, and Communication Technologies (CE2CT 2026) was held on July 02–04, 2026 at Graphic Era Hill University (GEHU), Bhimtal Campus, Nainital, Uttarakhand, India. Technically co-sponsored by the IEEE Uttar Pradesh (UP) Section, the conference successfully brought together leading academic scientists, researchers, scholars, and industry professionals from around the globe.

Following the tremendous success of the 2025 Inaugural Edition (where 261 papers were published in IEEE Xplore within a record 40 days), CE2CT 2026 expanded its scope to feature 5 pivotal tracks including Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Electronics & Communication, Robotics & Automation, and Sustainable Computing. All presented papers are submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore and indexed in SCOPUS.
