Presentation Guideline

DOWNLOAD CE2CT 2027 PRESENTATION TEMPLATE (.PPTX)
Looking for the previous edition template? Download CE2CT-2026 Presentation Template (Archive)

  1. General Instructions:
    • Presentation: At least one author of each registered paper is required to deliver a presentation at the scheduled time.
    • Punctuality: Join the session at least 10 minutes before your scheduled time to ensure smooth proceedings and avoid technical issues.
  2. Presentation Format:
    • Slide Requirements:
      • Prepare a PowerPoint Presentation (PPT) as per the Provided TEMPLATE only with a minimum of 12 slides and a maximum of 25 slides.
      • Keep slides visually appealing with a balance of text, images, graphs, and bullet points.
      • Use readable fonts and maintain consistency in formatting throughout the slides.
    • Time Allocation:
      • Total presentation time is 15 minutes.
        • 12 minutes for the presentation.
        • 3 minutes for the Q&A session.
