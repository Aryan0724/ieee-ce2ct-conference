Submission Note

Interdisciplinary contributions spanning across multiple tracks are strongly encouraged. All papers will undergo rigorous double-blind peer review by international domain experts.

All Submissions must strictly adhere to the IEEE template and be submitted through the conference submission portal. Authors should follow the detailed descriptions in the paper template to prepare their manuscripts. The complete papers must adhere to the IEEE paper template and should consist of a minimum of 4 pages, with a maximum limit of 6 pages. The official IEEE paper templates (Doc/LaTeX) can be accessed from the IEEE author center: https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html



PAPER SUBMISSION


CMT Acknowledgement: The Microsoft CMT service is used for managing the peer-reviewing process for this conference. This service is provided for free by Microsoft and they bear all expenses, including costs for Azure cloud services as well as for software development and support.


All submissions must be submitted electronically in PDF format using CMT through the conference website. Authors are advised to not submit papers directly via email. Authors must adhere to the policies of CE2CT-2027 when submitting a paper. We advise authors to submit their paper by taking into account the following conference Policy on plagiarism:

  • The overall similarity rate of a conference paper submission must be less than 15% including reference material.
  • The overall similarity rate of a conference paper submission must be less than 10% excluding reference material.
  • The similarity rate for a single source should not be more than 2% if any.
  • The AI-generated text must be strictly 0% (Not *%).
  • An individual may not be listed as an author or co-author on more than two papers. Authors must confirm that all author names, affiliations, and email addresses are correct. The authors are solely responsible for any incorrect or mismatched information.
  • Please ensure that all authors are duly added in the CMT portal as well as correctly listed in the manuscript prior to submission.


CMT Paper Submission Portal (CE2CT 2027)

The Microsoft CMT paper submission portal link for CE2CT 2027 will be updated soon. Full paper submissions are open till 31 January 2027.



🧪 Join the CE2CT 2027 Peer Review Panel

We invite experienced researchers, academicians, industry persons and professionals to be a part of the peer review process for CE2CT-2027.

If you are interested in contributing to the academic integrity and quality of this conference, the reviewer registration form will be updated soon. Interested reviewers may also express their interest by writing to the secretariat at: ce2ct2027@gmail.com.

Your support as a reviewer will help ensure a fair, rigorous, and high-standard evaluation process for all submitted papers. Reviewers will receive an official certificate of acknowledgment for their valuable contribution.



📢 Call for Special Sessions – CE2CT 2027

We welcome proposals for Special Sessions from researchers and professionals interested in organizing focused thematic tracks under the umbrella of CE2CT 2027.

If you are interested in conducting a special session, please submit your proposal and session outline to the conference secretariat at ce2ct2027@gmail.com.

Special Sessions offer a great opportunity to bring together experts on emerging topics and foster collaboration within specific research communities.



PAPER ACCEPTANCE


Reviewers will evaluate all submitted papers, and acceptance will be announced afterwards. Authors of accepted papers will be notified, and then authors have to complete the Paper Registration. For more information, refer to the Registration & Fees page.

Note for Authors with accepted papers:

  • Authors are required to pay the registration fee (NON – REFUNDABLE).
  • An individual may not be listed as an author or co-author on more than two papers, irrespective of authorship position.
  • IEEE requires that an author of every published paper sign a copyright agreement.
  • The IEEE Plagiarism policy will be rigorously enforced, and any violation of this policy, detected at any stage, will result in the rejection of the paper.
  • Authors of accepted papers will submit the Camera-Ready-Copy (CRC) of their accepted papers.
  • All the accepted and presented papers will be submitted for inclusion into IEEE Xplore subject to meeting IEEE Xplore’s scope and quality requirements.
