Conference Scope

The scope of the "3rd International Conference on Advances in Computer Science, Electrical, Electronics and Communication Technologies" (CE2CT 2027) encompasses cutting-edge advancements, theoretical foundations, and real-world innovations across interdisciplinary computing, electrical systems, and communication engineering. Following the resounding success of the Inaugural Edition (2025) and the Second Edition (CE2CT 2026, IEEE Record #68422), CE2CT 2027 continues to provide an esteemed platform for researchers, academicians, and industry pioneers worldwide.

We invite original research contributions related to the following pivotal fields:

  • Computer Science and Engineering with theory and applications in different areas
  • Electrical Engineering Technologies with theory and applications in different areas
  • Electronics and Communication Technologies with theory and applications in different areas
  • Interdisciplinary and Emerging Technologies
  • Networking and Broadcast Technology
  • Engineering Profession
  • Computing and Processing
  • Robotics and Control Systems
  • Power, Energy and Industry Applications
  • Signal Processing and Analysis, Components, Circuits, Devices and Systems
  • Computing and Processing
  • Engineered Materials
  • Robotics and Control Systems
  • Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR)
  • Bioinformatics and Computational Biology
  • Neurotechnology and Brain-Computer Interfaces
  • Smart Manufacturing and Industry 4.0
  • Multidisciplinary Modeling and Simulation.

The conference might serve as a platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students to present their latest research findings, exchange ideas, and discuss emerging trends and challenges in these interdisciplinary fields.
