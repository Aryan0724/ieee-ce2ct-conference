Venue

The Graphic Era Hill University (GEHU) Bhimtal Campus is situated in Bhimtal, Nainital, Uttarakhand, India. Here's some information about the venue:

Location: Bhimtal is a picturesque town located in the Nainital district of Uttarakhand, known for its natural beauty and serene surroundings. It is nestled in the foothills of the Himalayas and is surrounded by lush greenery and the stunning Bhimtal Lake.

Campus Infrastructure: The GEHU Bhimtal Campus likely offers modern infrastructure and facilities conducive to academic and research activities.

Scenic Setting: Being situated in the Himalayan foothills, the GEHU Bhimtal Campus provides a tranquil and inspiring environment for learning, research, and networking. The natural beauty of the surroundings can enhance the conference experience for attendees.

Accessibility:: Bhimtal is well-connected by road and is easily accessible from major cities like National Capital Delhi and State Capital Dehradun. The nearest railway station is Kathgodam, which is approximately 22 kilometers away, and the nearest airport is Pantnagar Airport, approximately 58 kilometers away.

Local Attractions: Participants attending the conference may also have the opportunity to explore the nearby attractions during their stay in Bhimtal. This could include visits to Bhimtal Lake, Nainital town, Naina Devi Temple, and other natural and cultural landmarks in the region.
